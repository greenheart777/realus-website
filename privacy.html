Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026
This policy applies to the Realus app for Meta Quest and to this website.

In short: Realus is an offline video editor. It does not require an account and does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal data. The videos and audio you edit stay on your headset.

01General information

Realus (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a virtual reality video editor for Meta Quest headsets. You can arrange video clips on a timeline, add layers and audio, preview the result and export a finished video. All editing happens locally on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the app works with, how it is used, and how you can delete it.

02Data we collect

We do not collect personal data. Realus has no user accounts, no registration, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not send your content or any information about you to us or to anyone else.

We do not request or collect:

  • your name, email address, phone number or other contact details;
  • payment information;
  • location data;
  • voice recordings or microphone input;
  • photos or recordings of your physical surroundings from the headset cameras;
  • biometric data, including eye, face or body tracking data;
  • usage statistics, crash analytics or advertising identifiers.

Data the app works with on your device

To function as a video editor, the app works with the following data locally on your headset only:

DataWhat happens to it
Video files you choose to editRead from your headset's storage so you can preview, trim and arrange them. They are never uploaded.
Audio files you import (MP3, WAV, OGG)A copy is saved in the app's private storage on your headset so it stays available in your project. Never uploaded.
Project state (clip order, cuts, layers, audio positions)Kept on your headset to let you continue editing.
Exported videosRendered on your headset and saved to its storage.
Hand and controller trackingProvided by the Meta Quest system in real time so you can grab and move objects. Realus uses it only for interaction while the app is running and does not record, store or transmit it.

03How data is used

The data listed above is used only to provide the app's features on your device: displaying and playing your clips, editing your project, mixing audio, rendering and saving the final video, and letting you interact with the interface using your hands or controllers.

We do not use any data for advertising, profiling, marketing or analytics, and we do not sell it.

04Permissions

Realus may ask for permission to access media or files on your headset. This is needed only to open the videos and audio you select and to save the videos you export. You can deny or revoke this permission at any time in your headset's settings. Without it, the app cannot open or save your media.

05Third parties

Realus does not share data with third parties and does not include third-party advertising, analytics or tracking services.

The app runs on Meta Quest and is distributed through the Meta Horizon Store. Meta may process information about your device, account and app usage under its own privacy policy. That processing is controlled by Meta, not by us.

06Data storage and deletion

Everything Realus works with is stored only on your headset. We do not keep any of your data on our servers, so there is nothing for us to hold or delete on our side.

You can delete your data at any time:

  • Remove clips or audio from a project inside the app.
  • Delete exported videos using the file manager on your headset.
  • Uninstall Realus to remove all of the app's private data from your headset, including imported audio copies and project state.

If you have any questions about your data or want help deleting it, contact us at the address below. We will reply within 30 days.

07Children's privacy

Realus does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Since the app collects no personal data, no data about children is collected either. Please follow Meta's age requirements for using Meta Quest devices.

08Security

Your media and projects never leave your headset through Realus, so they are protected by your device's own security. We recommend keeping your headset's software up to date.

09Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy, for example when new features are added. The current version will always be published on this page with a new effective date. If a future version of the app begins to collect any data, this policy will be updated to describe it before that version is released.
