Why Realus

Realus turns video editing into something physical. Clips are objects you can grab, carry and drop onto a timeline floating in front of you. No tiny buttons. No nested menus. Just you and your footage.

01 — EDIT WITH YOUR HANDS

Grab it. Drop it. Done.

Drop a clip onto the timeline and its neighbours slide apart to make room. Trim the ends, split it into parts and reorder freely.

02 — LAYERS

Up to four video layers.

Upper layers cover lower ones, perfect for cutaways, inserts and overlays. Add or remove a layer with a single button.

03 — SOUND

Music, voice, effects.

A dedicated audio track sits under the video. Drop a clip where you want it and it lands at that exact moment. Overlaps stack into lanes on their own.

04 — PREVIEW & EXPORT

Watch it. Ship it.

Play back every layer with synced sound at any time, then export the finished video right on your headset. No PC required.
